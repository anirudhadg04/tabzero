## 🚀 Quick Installation Guides

### 1. 🌐 Browser Extension (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, Firefox)

#### For Chromium-Based Browsers (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, Arc):
1. **Download:** [Click here to download the extension archive (`browser-extension.zip`)](https://github.com/anirudhadg04/tabzero/archive/refs/heads/browser-extension.zip) and extract it on your computer.
2. **Open Extensions Page:** Navigate to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`, `brave://extensions`).
3. **Enable Developer Mode:** Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top right corner.
4. **Load Unpacked:** Click **Load unpacked** and select the `dist/` directory inside the extracted folder.
5. **Pin & Enjoy:** Pin **tabzero.** to your toolbar for immediate mindful focus and deflection protection.

#### For Mozilla Firefox:
1. **Download:** [Download the extension archive](https://github.com/anirudhadg04/tabzero/archive/refs/heads/browser-extension.zip) and extract it.
2. **Open Debugging:** Navigate to `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` in Firefox.
3. **Load Add-on:** Click **Load Temporary Add-on...**.
4. **Select Manifest:** Select `dist-firefox/manifest.json` from the extracted folder.

---

### 2. 📱 Android Mobile Application

1. **Download APK:** [Download `tabzero-v1.0.1.apk`](https://github.com/anirudhadg04/tabzero/releases/download/v1.0.1/tabzero-v1.0.1.apk) from the [Releases page](https://github.com/anirudhadg04/tabzero/releases).

3. **Open tabzero.:** Launch the app to track movement, set daily focus goals, and create distraction-free digital boundaries.

## 🌿 The tabzero. Philosophy

Technology should serve human intention, not exploit human attention. **tabzero.** bridges desktop browsing and mobile living with a shared philosophy rooted in calm, autonomy, and real-world presence:

- **Quiet Friction, Never Punishment:** We reject hostile lockers and punitive alarms. When compulsive habits kick in, tabzero replaces infinite scrolling and infinite tabs with peaceful nature sanctuaries—inviting you to pause, breathe, and step away on your own terms.
- **Autonomy Over Algorithms:** No rigid categories, arbitrary quotas, or pre-baked schedules. Your offline time and pledges are entirely user-defined, dynamic, and flexible, supported by persistent 5-step undo/redo so you remain in complete control.
- **Rooted in the Physical World:** Traditional wellness trackers often keep you staring at screens analyzing screen time charts. tabzero connects with live weather intelligence to offer context-aware nudges—reminding you to walk under open skies when it's bright or enjoy mindful indoor focus when it rains.
- **Local-First & Uncompromised Privacy:** Your focus, screen time, and habits are sacred personal data. Both the browser extension and mobile application operate entirely on-device—no cloud lock-in, no telemetry, and no surveillance.
